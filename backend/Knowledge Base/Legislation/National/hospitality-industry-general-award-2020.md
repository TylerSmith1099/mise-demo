# Hospitality Industry (General) Award 2020 (MA000009) — Operational Summary

> ORIGINAL SUMMARY. Paraphrased operational guidance on the Hospitality Industry
> (General) Award 2020. Not the verbatim award. Applies in all states.
> DEPLOY FLAG: load the current published award text and rates before production
> sign-off (rates change annually).

## Coverage and classifications
The award covers employers and employees in the hospitality industry, with
classification levels from introductory and Level 1 through to managerial
supervisory levels. The classification determines the minimum rate. Each employee
must be classified according to the work they are actually required to perform.

## Penalty rates and overtime
Work on evenings, weekends, and public holidays attracts penalty rates set by the
award. Overtime applies for hours worked beyond the rostered ordinary hours or
outside the spread of hours. Casual employees receive a casual loading in place of
paid leave entitlements, plus applicable penalties.

## Breaks, allowances, and rosters
The award sets meal breaks and rest pauses based on shift length, and a range of
allowances (for example, a split-shift allowance, laundry allowance, and a higher-
duties allowance when relieving in a higher classification). Rosters and changes to
rosters must be made in accordance with the award's notice requirements.

## Minimum engagement and junior rates
Part-time and casual employees have a minimum engagement period per shift. Junior
employees are paid a percentage of the adult rate according to age, except where
performing work that requires an adult rate (such as supervising or selling
liquor where the law requires an adult).
