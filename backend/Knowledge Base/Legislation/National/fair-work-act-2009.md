## National Employment Standards (NES)
The NES are minimum entitlements for national-system employees, including maximum
weekly hours, requests for flexible working arrangements, parental leave, annual
leave, personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave, public holidays, notice of
termination and redundancy pay, and the Fair Work Information Statement. Awards and
agreements cannot provide less than the NES.

## Hours of work and breaks
An employee's ordinary hours and entitlement to breaks are set by the applicable
award or agreement, subject to the NES maximum weekly hours and reasonable
additional hours. Rosters must respect minimum break requirements between shifts.

## Unfair dismissal and general protections
Eligible employees are protected from unfair dismissal and from adverse action
taken for a prohibited reason (such as exercising a workplace right). A fair
process — valid reason, notice, and an opportunity to respond — must be followed
for performance or conduct dismissals.

## Record keeping and pay slips
Employers must keep prescribed employee records (hours, pay, leave) for seven years
and issue pay slips within one working day of payment. Records must be legible, in
English, and not falsified.
