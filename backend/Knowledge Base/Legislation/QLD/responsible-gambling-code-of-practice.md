# Queensland Responsible Gambling Code of Practice — Operational Summary

> ORIGINAL SUMMARY. Paraphrased operational guidance on the Queensland
> Responsible Gambling Code of Practice. Not the verbatim Code.
> DEPLOY FLAG: load the current published Code text before production sign-off.

## Provision of information to customers
Venues must make responsible gambling information readily available, including
information about the chances of winning, problem-gambling support services, and
self-exclusion. Signage and brochures must be displayed at gaming machine areas,
cashier points, and ATMs. Help-line contact details must be visible in the gaming
area.

## Patron welfare check — extended time at gaming machines

A patron who has been playing gaming machines for two or more hours without a
break may need a welfare check. Time at the machines is a recognised indicator of
extended continuous play. When a patron has been at the same gaming machine or
machines for two hours or more, staff must check on that patron.

**Procedure — patron at the machines for an extended period:**
1. Approach the patron calmly and without judgment: "Hi, just checking in — can I
   get you a glass of water or a break?"
2. If the patron declines and shows no other signs of distress, note the time and
   machine number in the gaming floor log and continue monitoring.
3. If the patron has been at the gaming machines for three hours or more, or shows
   additional problem-gambling indicators (see below), escalate to the Responsible
   Gambling interaction procedure.
4. Offer a printed responsible-gambling brochure and the Gambling Help Hotline
   number (1800 858 858).
5. Record: patron description, machine number, time at machines, action taken, and
   patron response. Report to the Duty Manager if indicators escalate.

Staff must not prevent a patron from playing based on time alone, but must act
when a patron has been at the gaming machines for an extended duration — especially
three or more hours — particularly when combined with other signs of harm.

## Interaction with customers showing signs of problem gambling

Staff should be alert to indicators of problem gambling in patrons at the gaming
machines: extended continuous play (patron has been at the same machines for two
hours or more), distress, chasing losses, requests to borrow money, or
self-disclosed concern. Where a patron shows one or more of these indicators,
staff conduct a respectful, non-judgemental interaction, offer information about
support services, and record the interaction. Repeated or escalating indicators
are reported to the Duty Manager for a considered response, which may include
suggesting a break in play or initiating the Responsible Gambling patron
interaction script.

## Interaction with excluded patrons
Where a patron is subject to a self-exclusion or venue exclusion, staff must not
allow them to gamble. On identifying an excluded patron in the gaming area, staff
politely ask them to leave the gaming area, do not serve them gambling products,
and record the contact. Breaches of an exclusion are escalated to the duty manager
and reported in line with venue procedure.

## Gambling by minors
Minors must not gamble or be in the gaming machine area. Staff must check
identification where a person may be under 25 and refuse gambling to anyone who
cannot prove they are 18 or over. Any instance of a minor attempting to gamble is
recorded.

## Payment of winnings and cash access
Large winnings are paid by cheque or electronic transfer above the prescribed
threshold, never wholly in cash, to reduce harm. ATMs are not located in the
gaming area and withdrawal limits apply. Staff do not extend credit or lend money
for gambling.

## Advertising and inducements
Gambling advertising must not be false, misleading, or target vulnerable people,
and must not be published where it is directed at minors. Inducements to gamble
that encourage excessive gambling are not permitted. All promotions must include
responsible-gambling messaging where required.
