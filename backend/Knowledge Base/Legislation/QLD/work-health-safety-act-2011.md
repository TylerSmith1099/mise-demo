# Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (QLD) — Operational Summary

> ORIGINAL SUMMARY. Paraphrased operational guidance on the Work Health and Safety
> Act 2011 (Qld). Not the verbatim statute.
> DEPLOY FLAG: load the full consolidated statute text before production sign-off.

## Primary duty of care
A person conducting a business or undertaking (PCBU) must ensure, so far as is
reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers and others affected by
the business. This includes a safe work environment, safe systems of work, and
adequate facilities, information, training, instruction, and supervision.

## Duties of workers and managers
Workers must take reasonable care for their own safety and that of others, comply
with reasonable instructions, and cooperate with safety policies. Managers
(officers) must exercise due diligence to ensure the PCBU complies, including
keeping up to date with hazards and ensuring resources for safety are available.

## Hazard identification and incident response
Hazards must be identified and risks controlled using the hierarchy of controls.
Where an incident occurs, the area is made safe, first aid is provided, and the
event is recorded. A notifiable incident — a death, serious injury or illness, or
dangerous incident — must be reported to the regulator immediately and the site
preserved so far as is reasonably practicable.

## Consultation and training
The PCBU must consult workers on matters affecting their health and safety and
provide the training needed to perform work safely, including manual handling,
glass and bottle handling, and managing aggressive patrons.

## Records and notifiable incidents
Records of notifiable incidents must be kept for the prescribed period. Staff must
know how to report a hazard or incident, who the responsible manager is, and the
location of first-aid and emergency equipment.
