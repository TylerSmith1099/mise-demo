# Liquor Act 1992 (QLD) — Operational Summary

> ORIGINAL SUMMARY. Paraphrased operational guidance on the Liquor Act 1992 (Qld).
> Not the verbatim statute.

## Responsible service of alcohol (RSA)
Licensees and staff must not sell or supply liquor to a person who is unduly
intoxicated. Service must be refused to an intoxicated person, and reasonable
steps taken to ensure they do not continue to consume liquor on the premises.
Staff serving liquor must hold current RSA training as required for the licence.

## Sale or supply to minors
Liquor must not be sold or supplied to a person under 18. Staff must ask for
acceptable proof of age where a person may be under 25 and refuse service if age
cannot be established. A minor must not be on licensed premises in a designated
area except as permitted (for example, dining with a responsible adult).

## Unduly intoxicated and disorderly patrons
A licensee must not permit unduly intoxicated, disorderly, or violent behaviour on
the premises. Staff should monitor patrons, intervene early, and remove or refuse
service to patrons who are intoxicated or disorderly. Incidents are recorded in
the incident register.

## Approved trading hours and licence conditions
Liquor may only be sold during the trading hours authorised by the licence and
subject to its conditions. Extended hours require approval. Staff must understand
the venue's specific licence conditions, including any conditions relating to the
gaming area, function areas, and entertainment.

## Safe transport and harm minimisation
Venues must take reasonable steps to support patron safety, including providing
information about safe transport options and water. Risk-based licensing and
community-safety obligations require management of patron numbers, noise, and the
area around the venue.

## Incident register and reporting
Serious incidents — refusals of service that escalate, ejections, injuries, and
police attendance — are recorded in the incident register with date, time, persons
involved, and action taken. Records are made available to investigators on request.
